# Code of conduct

We want participation in Coeval to be respectful, constructive, and safe.

Contributors are expected to:

- discuss ideas and code without personal attacks;
- assume good intent while remaining specific about technical concerns;
- welcome people regardless of background, identity, or level of experience;
- respect privacy and never post another person's sensitive information; and
- stop behavior that a maintainer identifies as disruptive or unsafe.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer rather than opening a public issue. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
